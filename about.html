Our Story

Built for the Demands of Global Commerce.

Founded in 2026 and headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, VECTRA International Logistics Ltd is a premium freight, trading, and supply chain solutions company.

We specialise in vehicle logistics from the USA, Europe, and Asia, moving high-value automobiles and commercial fleets with full documentation. Beyond vehicles, we operate across electric last-mile delivery, strategic procurement, freight & customs, and supply chain advisory.

Our clients range from automobile dealers and corporate fleets to manufacturers, online retailers, banks and public-sector agencies. What they share is a need for certainty in markets that rarely offer it.

“Where standard freight forwarders offer transit, VECTRA offers architecture.”

Our Mission

To deliver flawless, end-to-end logistics and trade solutions that connect Nigerian enterprise to global opportunity, moving vehicles, commodities, and capital across borders with absolute precision, unwavering integrity, and zero tolerance for supply chain failure.
