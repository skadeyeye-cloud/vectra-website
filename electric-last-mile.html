Overview

Sustainable, precision-tracked urban delivery across Nigerian cities.

VECTRA's electric fleet moves parcels, prescriptions, documents and orders across the city without burning fuel. Every rider is assigned and insured, every trip is GPS-tracked, and every delivery ends with proof. Your customers get speed and you get certainty.

What’s included

  • E-bike delivery fleet: zero emissions, low cost
  • Real-time tracking via dedicated dispatch platform
  • Same-day and scheduled delivery windows
  • Coverage across Abuja, Lagos & expanding cities
  • Green logistics: reduced carbon per delivery

Service Breakdown

What We Deliver.

The Fleet

GPS-tracked cargo e-bikes and electric three-wheelers optimised for Nigerian urban terrain, rider-assigned and insured.

How It Works

Order by call, WhatsApp, or API. Dispatch assigns the nearest rider with a real-time ETA and proof of delivery.

Business Use Cases

E-commerce fulfilment, pharma last-mile, food delivery, document courier, and bank instrument dispatch.

Why Electric

Lower cost per delivery, zero fuel dependency and reduced maintenance, aligned with corporate ESG goals.

Our Process

How It Works.

A clear, accountable sequence from first conversation to final delivery.

  1. Step 01

    Place an order

    Book by phone, WhatsApp, or plug our dispatch into your store through the API.

  2. Step 02

    Smart dispatch

    The nearest available rider is assigned and heads straight to pickup.

  3. Step 03

    Live tracking

    A real-time ETA is shared with you and your customer.

  4. Step 04

    Proof of delivery

    Every drop is confirmed on handover, so nothing goes missing without a trace.

  5. Step 05

    Reporting

    Delivery records to support reconciliation and your ESG reporting.
