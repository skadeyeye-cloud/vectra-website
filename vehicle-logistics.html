Overview

Importing precision-engineered vehicles across three continents.

From a single luxury SUV to a full corporate fleet, VECTRA sources, ships, clears and delivers vehicles into Nigeria with every document in order. We buy through auctions, dealerships and OEM channels in the USA, UK, Germany, Japan and the UAE, and we stay responsible until the keys are in your hand.

What’s included

  • Sourcing from USA, UK, Germany, Japan & UAE
  • Full documentation: Bill of Lading, customs, duty
  • Insured transit with real-time cargo tracking
  • Port clearance & final delivery across Nigeria
  • Dealer & corporate fleet partnerships

Our Process

How It Works.

A clear, accountable sequence from first conversation to final delivery.

  1. Step 01

    Brief & specification

    Share the make, model, budget and timeline. We confirm the specification, sourcing channel and a landed-cost estimate.

  2. Step 02

    Sourcing & inspection

    We inspect and procure from auctions, dealerships or OEMs, with a pre-shipment report before you commit.

  3. Step 03

    Insured shipping

    Vehicles are booked, insured and tracked in real time from the origin port to Nigeria.

  4. Step 04

    Customs & clearing

    Our NCS brokerage team handles Form M, PAAR, duty and terminal release at Apapa, Tin Can and Onne.

  5. Step 05

    Delivery & handover

    Final delivery anywhere in Nigeria, with pre-delivery inspection and a complete document pack.
