Overview

Sourcing the right goods from the right suppliers, globally.

Buying internationally shouldn't mean trusting a stranger with your capital. VECTRA identifies, vets and manages suppliers for you, then carries the order through inspection, shipping, customs and delivery, with itemised landed costs quoted upfront.

What’s included

  • Global supplier identification & vetting
  • Specification development & RFQ management
  • Price benchmarking and negotiation
  • Pre-shipment inspection & quality assurance
  • Full import documentation & customs management

Our Process

How It Works.

A clear, accountable sequence from first conversation to final delivery.

  1. Step 01

    Specification & RFQ

    We turn your requirement into a clear specification and run the request for quotation.

  2. Step 02

    Supplier vetting

    Registration, track record and quality are verified, including factory audits where needed.

  3. Step 03

    Benchmark & negotiate

    Prices are benchmarked and terms negotiated in your favour before a PO is issued.

  4. Step 04

    Production & inspection

    We monitor production and inspect goods before they ship.

  5. Step 05

    Ship, clear & deliver

    Export docs, freight, customs and final delivery under one accountable team.
