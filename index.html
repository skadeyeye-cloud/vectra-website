About VECTRA

Global Logistics.
Real Opportunities.

Founded in 2026 and headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, VECTRA International Logistics Ltd is a premium freight, trading, and supply chain solutions company built for the demands of global commerce.

We specialise in vehicle logistics from the USA, Europe, and Asia, moving high-value automobiles and commercial fleets with full documentation. Beyond vehicles, we operate across electric last-mile delivery, strategic procurement, freight & customs, and supply chain advisory.
